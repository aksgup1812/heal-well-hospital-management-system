How do I book an appointment?

Choose a doctor or service, then sign in to the patient portal to select an available date and time.

Can I cancel my appointment?

Yes. Open My Appointments in the patient portal and choose Cancel. The slot becomes available again.

How do I find a doctor?

Use the search bar or browse our featured specialists and departments above.

How can I access my lab reports?

Sign in to your patient portal to see laboratory status and results connected to your account.

Is emergency care available 24/7?

Yes. For urgent assistance, call 108 or visit the emergency department immediately.
